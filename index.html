Syncing

One-time imports

Hardcover

Hardcover library

Keeps your Hardcover library and one category in step. Books already in it are matched by title and author. When a book changed on both sides, the newer change wins, and Waypage starting or finishing one counts as a change here. Deleting a book here never deletes it on Hardcover. Uses your Hardcover token from Media lookups: it needs access to your library, so make one with only that if Hardcover lets you pick.

AniList

Your lists

Keeps your lists and LifeLog in step. Planning comes in as backlog items, Watching as in progress with its episode count, Completed goes to your timeline on the day AniList has it finished, and Paused or Dropped come in dropped. When a title changed on both sides, the newer change wins; with no time to go by, the further one does (Completed over Watching, more episodes over fewer). Completed titles AniList has no dates for wait for you to review. Removing something here never removes it on AniList. Needs no key, but until you sign in below the lists must be public (AniList → Settings → Lists → untick "Set anime/manga list to private"). Set one side to "Don't import" to sync only the other.

Send changes to AniList

Sends your episode counts, starts, finishes and drops back to AniList. Once: on anilist.co open Settings → Developer → Create new client, name it LifeLog, set the redirect URL to https://anilist.co/api/v2/oauth/pin and save. Paste its ID above, press Sign in, approve, and paste the token AniList shows you here. The token lasts a year and can change anything on your AniList account, so it syncs like your other keys but is left out of exports; delete the client on AniList to cut it off. Titles you add here after signing in are added to your AniList lists too.

Stremio

Stremio account

Reads your Stremio library and never changes it. Your library comes in as backlog items, what you're partway through as in progress with the episode you're on, and watched movies go to your timeline on the day you last played them (Stremio keeps only the last play). Everything goes through the review; an automatic check only offers it. Titles are matched on TMDB when you have a TMDB key. Your password is only used to sign in: the key Stremio sends back syncs like your other keys and is left out of exports. It can change anything on your Stremio account, and Sign out ends it. This is the API Stremio's own apps use; it isn't public and may change.

Up next row in Stremio

Add to Stremio

A row on Stremio's home screen with the movies and shows waiting in your Backlog, starred first. Tap one and it opens in Stremio as usual. It's an add-on your proxy worker serves at a secret address: LifeLog sends it only each title's IMDb id, name and poster, and anyone with the add-on link could see that list. Add to Stremio opens the Stremio app; on the web, copy the link and paste it into Stremio's add-on search. Shows from TMDB need your TMDB key to find their IMDb id.

Steam

Steam wishlist

Your wishlist must be public (Steam → Privacy Settings → Game details), and this goes through the CORS proxy on Media lookups.

Steam games you've played

A one-time backfill: every game with time in it becomes a Timeline entry in the month you last played it, with the hours as its length, in the wishlist's category. Steam doesn't know what you finished, so you review the list: two hours or more starts ticked. Game details must be public, like the wishlist.

Retry looks up again the items still called "Steam app <id>" (usually Steam rate-limiting a large first sync) and fixes the ones that resolve.

Backfill fills in what Steam-synced games lack: rating, length and year from RAWG (needs a RAWG key), and Steam's description (needs the proxy). Never touches title, cover or App ID.

Game times and scores

For games with a Steam App ID: the Main Story, Main + Extras and Completionist times from How Long to Beat, and the review scores, through Augmented Steam's API and the CORS proxy. Main Story becomes the game's length unless you pinned it. Only the App ID is sent. With IGDB keys set, IGDB's time-to-beat stands in where How Long to Beat has nothing, and for games picked from IGDB that aren't on Steam. A game is checked again once its times are older than the interval you pick here.

Letterboxd

On Letterboxd: Settings → Import & Export → Export your data. Pick the zip it gives you (or its CSV files). Your diary and watched films become Timeline entries on the day you saw them, with your stars; the watchlist goes to the Backlog. You review the list before anything comes in.

Google Calendar

Shows your Google Calendar's events on the Timeline's calendar, read only: LifeLog never changes your calendar. Tap an event to copy it into LifeLog as an entry, a task or a note. Google shows a "Google hasn't verified this app" page first: choose Advanced, then go on to LifeLog.

Not available here: Google sign-in needs the app, or the web app at its https address.

Calendars to show

The events stay on this device and aren't synced with the rest of your data, so each device connects on its own. In a browser the sign-in lasts an hour; after that the calendar shows the last copy until Sync now.

Google Wallet

When you pay with this phone, LifeLog offers to add it. Add files it where that place went last time; tapping the offer opens the form filled in. A payment in another currency only opens the form, for its rate.

Android has to give LifeLog notification access for this, which lets it see your notifications. It reads only Google Wallet's, and nothing leaves the phone. If Android says the setting is restricted, open LifeLog's App info, tap ⋮ and choose Allow restricted settings, then try again.
