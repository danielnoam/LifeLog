### Added
- Reading log from Waypage (Settings → Imports → Waypage reading): with Waypage's Share reading activity on, LifeLog reads what you read there every few minutes, with a read-only token for your Waypage sync repo. A book, audiobook, comic or serial already in your Backlog starts there and shows how far you are (62%, or ch 31/40), one you don't have comes in by itself, and finishing it in Waypage puts it on your Timeline on that day. You pick which category each kind goes to; articles stay out unless you pick one.
- One that's already on your Timeline (a re-read), or matches a backlog title by another author, waits in a review list there: Same one, Add, or Never.
- Reading and listening minutes from Waypage fill the minutes habit you pick, on top of the minutes you add by hand.
- The AI bridge has `lifelog_waypage` (what's being read, what finished, the review list) and `lifelog_waypage_review` (answer a review row).
