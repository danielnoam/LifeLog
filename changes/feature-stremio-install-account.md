### Added
- Settings → Connections → Stremio → Up next: **Add to my Stremio account** puts the LifeLog: Up next add-on in your Stremio account's add-ons, so Stremio on a TV (or anywhere you're signed in) gets it with no link to type. Needs you signed in to Stremio in LifeLog; your other add-ons are kept as they are. The web proxy passes Stremio's add-on list calls on (redeploys itself from main).
