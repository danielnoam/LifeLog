### Added
- The Tasks widget shows more of each task, as the app does: the repeat in words, whether it has a reminder, its deadline (red once past), its own category, its tags, and the first line of its description. List headings show theirs too.
- The Tasks widget's settings can turn off the tick on each list's name, the details under each task, and the description line.

### Changed
- The Tasks widget's ticks are round on Android 12 and up, as in the app.
