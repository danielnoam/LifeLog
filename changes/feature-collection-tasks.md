### Changed
- A task list in a collection shows its tasks on its card: open ones first, then the ticked ones struck through, six at most with "N more", and each one ticks right there. An empty list says "Nothing here." instead of "All done".
- A task list's page has a tick on every row, and the list itself has its own tick on its page and on its collection card (ticking it ticks every row), so you can tick and untick there too. Works in English and Hebrew, on phone and computer, in the web, Windows and Android apps.
