### Added
- Investments: log your buys and sales on a ticker for a Holding (shares, average cost, value and gain, what sales made), with buy and sell marks and a dotted "your average" line on the chart, and a "You hold" total over Watching.
- Investments: Compare under Pins shows the change between any two pins, or a pin and the latest close.
- Investments: a notification when a ticker closes at or under its target (Settings → Notifications → Investment targets), checked at launch, on coming back and in Android's background refresh.
- Investments: tap the chart to hold a day (Pin this day, Buy on this day), then drag or hover to read the move from it.
