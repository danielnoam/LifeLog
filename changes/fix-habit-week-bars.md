### Changed
- Habit grids have smaller cells (at most 22px) spread across the card, faint lines between the days, and a ring around today's cell. The day letters are centred, so M and W line up with the rest.

### Removed
- The thin "week met" bar on weekly goals. Over or under the grid it looked like a row of cut-off cells and made those cards taller than the rest; the streak and Insights still say which weeks were met.
