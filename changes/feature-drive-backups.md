### Added
- Google Drive backups, in Settings → Sync beside GitHub: connect your Google account, then Back up now or let LifeLog back up on its own every day, 3 days, week, 2 weeks or month while it's open. Each backup is a copy of everything (entries, notes, boards and the watchlist, without your API keys) in a LifeLog folder in your Drive; the last three are kept, plus one a week old when backups run more often. Restore puts a backup's data back as a new save, so History can undo it, and keeps this device's keys. LifeLog can see only the files it made in your Drive. Works in the browser, the Android app and the Windows app.
